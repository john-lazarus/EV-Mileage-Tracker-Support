# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 17, 2026**

EV Mileage Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Data stored by the app

Vehicle names, charging sessions, odometer readings, preferences, and calculated insights are stored locally on your device. The app does not use an account, advertising SDK, analytics service, or cloud database.

## Backups

Backup export is optional and initiated by you. When you export a backup, you choose where it is saved or shared. The selected destination is governed by that service's privacy practices.

## Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites and does not display advertising.

## Retention and deletion

You control the records stored in the app. You can delete individual vehicles and their records in the app. Removing the app also removes its local data unless you previously exported a backup.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the change is released.

## Contact

For privacy questions, email [Ronnie.lazarus@gmail.com](mailto:Ronnie.lazarus@gmail.com). Do not include vehicle records or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to answer your request.
