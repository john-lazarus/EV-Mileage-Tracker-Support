# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 11, 2026**

EV Mileage Tracker is a vehicle record-keeping app. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal data. The optional trip planner uses online services, described in Trip planner below.

## Data stored by the app

Vehicle records, preferences, calculated insights, and imported Documents wallet files are stored in private app storage on your device. On iOS, when your iCloud account is available, the app also automatically attempts to save its latest full backup to your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit service. That backup contains vehicle records, portable preferences, and Documents wallet files. A failed iCloud backup does not prevent local saves. This backup supports reinstall recovery; it is not a history of every revision or a promise of synchronization between devices. No app account is required.

## Backups and transfers

On iOS and Android, you can protect a full backup export with a password before choosing where it is saved or shared. Password protection applies to that exported file. The automatic iOS iCloud backup is created separately and is not protected with your export password. An unencrypted export contains readable records and documents. Your chosen destination's privacy practices apply to the copy you save or share.

If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may copy eligible vehicle records, vehicle details, Documents wallet files, app preferences, and the document-lock preference through the backup service configured on your device. The current app rules include only the main record file, wallet manifest and files, app preferences, and the local document-security preferences. They exclude temporary sharing, staging, restore artifacts, and other preference files. Actual backup and recovery depend on your Android version, device, selected provider, settings, and available quota; they are not guaranteed. This process is controlled by Android and the selected backup provider.

## Trip planner

The trip planner is optional. It shows a map, finds destinations and plans routes with charging stops using Google Maps Platform.

The map is shown with Google's Maps SDK. Google states that the SDK collects technical data such as crash and performance data, an SDK identifier and how the map is used. If you allow location access, your position is shown on the map.

When you search for a destination, plan a route, or open a charger, the app sends the search text, the route's start, destination and stops, and your device's coordinates if you choose Current location, to the developer's trip-planning service. The service passes the request to Google Maps Platform and returns Google's answer to the app. The service does not store your searches, locations or routes. Nothing is sent until you search or plan a route.

The app creates a random ID for each installation. Its first 8 characters are shown in Settings as your Support code. The service keeps this ID, the platform (iOS or Android), daily and monthly counts of trip-planner requests to apply usage limits, and, if you subscribe, your subscription status and the store's purchase reference. It does not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Before the service accepts requests, the app uses Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity to confirm that it is a genuine copy of the app.

If you add your own Google key, the app sends trip-planner requests directly to Google with that key and does not use the developer's service for them. The key is stored in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore and is sent only to Google. Your own agreement with Google applies to those requests.

Trip Planner Plus and Pro are auto-renewing subscriptions sold through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment.

Location access is optional. It is used only for Current location as a route start and for the map's my-location button. You can plan routes without it.

Google's handling of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

To have the service's record for your installation deleted, email the address below with your Support code.

## Tracking

EV Mileage Tracker contains no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Retention and deletion

You control the vehicle records and documents stored in the app. You can delete records and Documents wallet files in the app. Removing the app deletes its local copy, but exported backups and copies stored by a device backup service or iCloud may remain. On an eligible iOS reinstall, the app can offer to restore the latest private iCloud backup. If you choose Start fresh and confirm deletion, the app attempts to delete that latest iCloud backup before continuing. Deleting local data or uninstalling the app alone does not establish that all backup copies have been deleted. Uninstalling the app does not delete the trip-planning service's record for that installation; email us with your Support code to have it deleted.

## Children

EV Mileage Tracker is intended for people who own, operate, or manage an electric vehicle. It is not directed to children under 13.
