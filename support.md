# Support

For help with EV Mileage Tracker:

1. Check that the iPhone is running iOS 18 or later.
2. Include the app version, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
3. Email [ronnie.lazarus.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ronnie.lazarus.dev@gmail.com).

Do not attach exported backups or include vehicle records, costs, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to diagnose the problem.

The app has no user account or server-side data to recover. If the app is removed without an exported backup, its local records cannot be restored.
